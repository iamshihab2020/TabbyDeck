  1. 0.2

    TabbyDeck 0.2.0

    Latest 23 October 2026

    Ready-made decks, a volume mixer, meeting keys, a trackpad, decks that follow your apps, and iPhone and iPad support.

    • Ready-made decks
    • Volume mixer
    • Meeting keys
    • Trackpad and keyboard
    • iPhone and iPad
    • Timers and Pomodoro
    Ready-made decks
    • Developer, Office, Streamer and Music decks. Pick them in the first-run guide, or from New profile in the editor; each one is its own profile, and you can delete it like any other.
    • Each deck gets only as many columns as fill its rows (25 keys make 5 by 5), and its app keys open the app found on your PC, with its own icon.
    • Keys for apps your PC does not have stay off the decks and come back once the app is installed. The keys after them close up, so no gap is left. The editor marks such keys "Not on this PC".
    On the phone
    • Swipe the keys left or right to change profile; dots by the profile name show where you are. A key the finger slides over never fires.
    • Trackpad and keyboard: a key opens a touchpad. One finger moves the pointer, a tap clicks, two fingers scroll or right-click, and a box types on the PC, with Enter and Backspace.
    • Live keys: CPU, memory, battery and the time, shown large on the key.
    • Timer and Pomodoro keys: press to start or pause, hold to reset. Every deck gets a message when the time is up or the break starts.
    • Add TabbyDeck to the home screen: it opens full screen from its own icon.
    • iPhone and iPad: the deck works in Safari on iOS 13 or newer, and from the home screen.
    Sound and media
    • Volume mixer: the Volume panel has a slider and mute for each app with sound, and a choice of output.
    • Switch sound output key: goes to a chosen output, or to the next one each press, and shows which one plays.
    • Now playing key: the title, the artist and the cover of what is playing. Tap to play or pause, hold for the next track.
    Meetings
    • Mute, camera, raise hand and leave keys, for the app you are in a call with: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Slack huddles or Discord, found by which app is using the microphone. Leave asks first.
    • The Camera key lights while an app uses the camera.
    Windows and apps
    • Do not disturb key: turns Windows notifications off and on, and lights while they are off.
    • Decks follow the app in front: give a profile its apps with Follow apps, and phones switch to it while one of them is in front, then back.
    • One button can open several apps together: in Apps on this PC, choose Pick several, tick the apps, then Add button.
    • Move window: snap a window to a half, maximize, minimize or send it to the other screen. It waits for an app that is still opening.
    • A Wait step, for buttons that open apps and then arrange them.
    • Open app or file opens folders too, and Switch to window can pick an app that is open now.
    Editor
    • Six key styles: Keycap, Sunken, Soft, Glass, Pad and Outline, for every profile at once or one at a time.
    • Put profiles in any order by dragging them; phones swipe through them in that order.
    • Steps read better: they show the app's name and the choice made.
    • Phone can't connect? Under the QR code, TabbyDeck checks the Wi-Fi, the firewall and VPNs on the PC, and lists what to try on the phone.
    Fixes
    • Security: text passed to Windows PowerShell can no longer end its quotes early with a curly quote, and phones cannot send app names with quotes in them.
    • Switch app goes to the app used before, on any screen.
    • Mute microphone could fail after the volume panel had been opened.

  2. 0.1

    TabbyDeck 0.1.0

    First release 22 October 2026

    The first release: any Android phone or tablet on your Wi-Fi becomes a deck of buttons for your Windows PC.

    • Android phones and tablets
    • Pairing with a code
    • Brightness per monitor
    • Bluetooth panel
    Everything in 0.1.0
    • Use any Android phone or tablet on your Wi-Fi as a deck of buttons for your Windows PC, in Chrome or Edge.
    • Pair a device by typing the 6-digit code it shows. Block or forget devices at any time.
    • Build decks in the editor: drag, resize, folders, profiles, colours, icons and images.
    • Add any installed app with its own icon from Apps on this PC.
    • Hotkeys, typing, mouse, app launching, links, media keys, volume, mute, microphone, sleep, lock and shut down.
    • Volume and brightness sliders, with brightness per monitor (laptop screens and DDC/CI monitors).
    • Bluetooth panel: turn Bluetooth on or off and connect paired headphones or speakers.
    • Night light, screen projection, screenshot, snip and emoji.
    • Keys show live state: Play / Pause, mute, mic, night light, Bluetooth, and volume and brightness levels.
    • Phones get thumb-sized keys that scroll; tablets show the whole deck.
    • A first-run guide and an editor tour.
    • Backup and restore.
    • Runs in the tray, starts with Windows, and uses under 80 MB of RAM.
    Download 0.1.0
