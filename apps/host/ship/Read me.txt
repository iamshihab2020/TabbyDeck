TabbyDeck 0.1.0 - turn a phone or tablet into buttons for your PC

Start
  1. Double-click tabbydeck.exe. The TabbyDeck window opens and an amber icon appears in the tray.
  2. If Windows asks whether TabbyDeck may use the network, tick Private networks and click Allow.

Connect your tablet
  1. Put the tablet on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. In TabbyDeck, open Devices. Scan the code with the tablet's camera, or type the address shown
     (it looks like http://192.168.1.20:8191) into Chrome on the tablet.
  3. The tablet shows six digits. Type them in the TabbyDeck window and click Allow.

The tablet cannot connect?
  Double-click "Allow phones to connect.cmd" and click Yes. This opens port 8191 in the Windows
  firewall, which is needed when your Wi-Fi is set to Public or the first prompt was dismissed.

Good to know
  - Closing the TabbyDeck window keeps TabbyDeck running in the tray. Right-click the tray icon to quit.
  - Pause actions in the tray menu stops every button until you turn it back on.
  - Your decks, images and logs live in %APPDATA%\tabbydeck. Settings > Save backup makes a copy.
  - Keep the three items in this folder together: tabbydeck.exe, tabbydeck-tray.exe and the koffi folder.
